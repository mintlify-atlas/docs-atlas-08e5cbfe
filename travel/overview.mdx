Travlr Getaways highlights three reef destinations that span very different corners of the world: a deep gorgonian-fan site off Colombo, a Great Barrier Reef fishing reef in Far North Queensland, and a shallow lagoonal patch reef in Bermuda's North Lagoon. Each site has its own character, depth profile, and set of conditions, so the right preparation and a knowledgeable local operator make all the difference.

## Planning Your Visit

Every reef destination requires some advance preparation. Conditions such as weather, visibility, currents, and protected-area zoning change regularly, and the best information always comes from operators who are on the water regularly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your destination">
    Review the individual destination pages to understand the depth, access requirements, and type of experience each reef offers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a qualified local operator">
    Contact a licensed operator based near the reef you want to visit. Local operators understand current zoning rules, weather windows, and protection requirements that no online guide can fully substitute for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check safety and etiquette guidelines">
    Before you depart, review the [Reef Etiquette Guide](/guide/reef-etiquette) and the [Water Safety Guide](/guide/water-safety) so you arrive prepared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm conditions on the day">
    Even with a booking confirmed, check in with your operator the morning of your trip. Sea state, visibility, and access can change overnight.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All reef visits featured on this site should be arranged through qualified local operators who are familiar with on-the-ground conditions, protected-area regulations, and reef-safe practices specific to each location. Please review the [Reef Etiquette Guide](/guide/reef-etiquette) and [Water Safety Guide](/guide/water-safety) before your trip.
</Note>
