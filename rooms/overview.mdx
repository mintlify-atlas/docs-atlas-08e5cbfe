Travlr Getaways offers three distinct room categories, each suited to a different travel style and group size. Whether you're after sweeping ocean views, a streamlined base for day-trippers, or extra space for a family retreat, there's an option that fits. Rates are per night, and each room type can be reserved directly through the booking form.
