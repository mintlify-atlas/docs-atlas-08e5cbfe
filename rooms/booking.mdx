Booking a room at Travlr Getaways is a straightforward process. You'll select your room type, enter your travel dates and guest count, and provide your contact details. Your request is routed through guest services, and you'll have a chance to review everything before your stay is finalized. If you haven't decided on a room yet, take a look at the [room types overview](/rooms/overview) to compare your options.

## Room Rates

All rates are per night and subject to availability at the time of booking.

| Room | Starting Rate |
|------|--------------|
| First Class Room | From $220/night |
| Deluxe Room | From $150/night |
| Suite Room | From $180/night |

<Info>
  Rates shown are starting prices. Final pricing is confirmed during the guest services review step after you submit your request.
</Info>

## Booking Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Your Room and Guests">
    Choose from three room types — **First Class Room** ($220/night), **Deluxe Room** ($150/night), or **Suite Room** ($180/night) — and select the number of guests in your party (1 to 4). The default is 2 guests.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Your Travel Dates">
    Enter your **check-in** and **check-out** dates. Both dates must be today or later. When you update the check-in date, the check-out minimum adjusts automatically to prevent invalid date ranges.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Your Contact Information">
    Provide your **first name**, **last name**, and **email address** — all required fields. You may also include a **phone number**, though this is optional.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Any Special Requests">
    Use the **Special Requests** field to share any accessibility needs, dining preferences, or other travel considerations. Examples include mobility accommodations, dietary restrictions, early check-in, or celebration setups. This information goes directly to the guest services team.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit and Review">
    Click **Continue Booking** to send your request to guest services. You will be taken to a review step where you can confirm all details before your reservation is finalized. Nothing is locked in until you complete that final confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The booking form routes through guest services via the contact portal. No charges are applied and no reservation is confirmed at the moment you click **Continue Booking** — you will have a full opportunity to review your request before your stay is committed.
</Note>

## What the Form Collects

The following fields make up the booking form. Fields marked as required must be completed before submitting.

<Accordion title="Reservation Details">
  | Field | Type | Required | Notes |
  |-------|------|----------|-------|
  | Room | Select | Yes | First Class, Deluxe, or Suite |
  | Guests | Select | Yes | 1–4 guests; defaults to 2 |
  | Check-in Date | Date | Yes | Cannot be in the past |
  | Check-out Date | Date | Yes | Auto-adjusts when check-in changes |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Guest Information">
  | Field | Type | Required | Notes |
  |-------|------|----------|-------|
  | First Name | Text | Yes | — |
  | Last Name | Text | Yes | — |
  | Email | Email | Yes | Used for confirmation correspondence |
  | Phone | Tel | No | Optional; helpful for guest services follow-up |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Special Requests">
  | Field | Type | Required | Notes |
  |-------|------|----------|-------|
  | Special Requests | Textarea | No | Accessibility, dining, or other travel needs |

  The placeholder text reads: *"Tell us about accessibility, dining, or other travel needs."* This field is optional, but filling it out before submitting ensures guest services can prepare your room and any arrangements ahead of arrival.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  For complex requests — such as booking adjoining rooms, arranging airport transfers, or coordinating a multi-night group stay — it's worth contacting guest services directly before using the booking form. Visit the [contact page](/contact) to reach the team.
</Tip>
