## Plan Your Stay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your room">
    Start with the [Rooms overview](/rooms/overview) to compare bed types, views, and nightly rates across all three accommodation categories. When you're ready, use the [Booking page](/rooms/booking) to select your dates and number of guests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the resort">
    Check the [Dining page](/meals/overview) to preview meal options, and browse [Destinations](/travel/overview) to identify reef excursions and water activities you'd like to add to your itinerary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the travel guide">
    The [Travel Guide](/guide/water-safety) covers water safety, kayaking preparation, responsible reef visits, and practical vacation tips — all drawn from authoritative sources so you can enjoy the coast safely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get in touch">
    Use the [Contact page](/contact) to ask about accessibility, special requests, or anything else before you arrive. Our guest-services team is ready to help you finalize the details.
  </Step>
</Steps>
