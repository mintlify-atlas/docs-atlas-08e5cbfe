## How to Reach Us

<Steps>
  <Step title="Decide what you need">
    Think about whether you are ready to reserve a specific room or whether you still have general questions about the resort, activities, meals, or accessibility. This will help you choose the right form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the right form">
    Use the [booking form](/rooms/booking) if you are ready to select a room type, choose check-in and check-out dates, and confirm the number of guests. For all other questions — activities, dining, accessibility, or anything else — use the general contact form and include your name, email, subject, and a message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include your dates and interests">
    Whether you are booking or just inquiring, include your preferred travel dates and any experiences that interest you, such as reef excursions, beach activities, or specific dining preferences. The more detail you share, the better we can tailor our recommendations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expect a reply from our team">
    Our guest-services team will review your message and respond with room availability, activity options, or answers to your questions. For reservation requests, you will have an opportunity to review all details before confirming your stay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="What to include in your message">
  To help us prepare the most helpful and accurate response, consider including the following information when you reach out:

  - **Preferred travel dates** — your ideal check-in and check-out dates, or a general window if dates are flexible
  - **Room preferences** — whether you are interested in the Deluxe ($150/night), Suite ($180/night), or First Class ($220/night) room
  - **Number of guests** — how many adults or children will be traveling with you (up to four guests per room)
  - **Accessibility or dietary needs** — any mobility, medical, or food-related requirements our team should know about in advance
  - **Activities of interest** — reef excursions, beach outings, snorkeling, or any other water activities you would like us to help arrange
  - **Questions about the resort** — anything else you would like to know about facilities, meals, policies, or transportation
</Accordion>

<Note>
  The [booking form](/rooms/booking) is the best starting point if you are ready to reserve a room. It collects all the details our team needs — room type, guest count, check-in and check-out dates, and contact information — so we can confirm your reservation quickly.
</Note>

<Tip>
  When filling out the booking form's Special Requests field, mention any reef excursion interests or activity preferences alongside accessibility and dining needs. Our team reviews every request and will follow up with tailored suggestions for your stay.
</Tip>
