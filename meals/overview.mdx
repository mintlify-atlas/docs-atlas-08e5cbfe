## Seafood Special

The Seafood Special is our signature dinner offering, celebrating the coastal setting of Travlr Getaways with fresh, thoughtfully prepared fish dishes. Sourced with the season in mind, each plate pairs ocean-fresh protein with vibrant accompaniments that keep things light and satisfying.

<Accordion title="Seared Salmon Plate — Dish Details">
  **Seared Salmon Plate**

  Salmon served with seasonal vegetables, rice, and a bright citrus-herb finish for a balanced coastal dinner. The citrus-herb sauce adds a zesty lift that complements the richness of the salmon without overpowering it, making this an ideal choice for guests who want something elegant but not heavy after a day at the beach.

  - **Protein:** Fresh-seared salmon fillet
  - **Sides:** Seasonal vegetables and steamed rice
  - **Finish:** Citrus-herb sauce
  - **Best for:** Dinner; pairs well with a light white wine or sparkling water
</Accordion>

<Info>
  Seafood menu offerings may rotate based on seasonal availability to ensure the freshest possible ingredients are always on your plate.
</Info>

## Sumptuous Desserts

No beach vacation is complete without a little indulgence. Our dessert selections are crafted to be unpretentious and delightful — the kind of sweet treat that feels just right after a long, happy day under the sun.

<Accordion title="Chocolate Ice Cream Sandwich — Dish Details">
  **Chocolate Ice Cream Sandwich**

  Chocolate ice cream layered between soft cookies and served chilled as a simple finish after a day at the beach. The cookies provide a soft, slightly chewy contrast to the cold, creamy ice cream, creating a dessert that is comforting without being overly rich. Served ready-to-enjoy, it's the kind of treat that brings out the simple joys of a beach holiday.

  - **Base:** Soft-baked cookies
  - **Filling:** Rich chocolate ice cream
  - **Serving temperature:** Chilled
  - **Best for:** Afternoon treat or post-dinner dessert
</Accordion>

<Note>
  Additional seasonal dessert options may be available depending on your stay dates. Ask our dining team about any rotating sweets or daily specials.
</Note>

## Buffet

Start your mornings right with our Breakfast Buffet — a welcoming, self-serve spread that gives every guest the freedom to build a plate that matches their appetite. Whether you prefer something light and fresh or a warm, filling meal before heading out, the buffet has you covered.

<Accordion title="Breakfast Buffet — What's Included">
  **Breakfast Buffet**

  A rotating selection of eggs, breakfast potatoes, fresh fruit, pastries, and hot and cold drinks with options for different appetites. The buffet is replenished throughout service to keep everything fresh, and the selection changes regularly so returning guests always have something new to look forward to.

  **Typical selections include:**
  - **Hot dishes:** Scrambled or prepared eggs, seasoned breakfast potatoes
  - **Fresh items:** Seasonal fruit, yogurt, and lighter fare
  - **Baked goods:** Pastries, muffins, and breads
  - **Beverages:** Hot coffee, tea, chilled juices, and water
  - **Best for:** Fueling up before a full day of beach activities or excursions
</Accordion>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Arrive During Buffet Hours">
    The breakfast buffet is served each morning during designated dining hours. Check with the front desk upon arrival for the exact schedule during your stay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build Your Plate">
    Help yourself to any combination of hot dishes, fresh items, and baked goods. Staff are available to assist if you have questions about any item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enjoy Your Morning">
    Find a comfortable seat in our dining area or take your coffee outside to enjoy the morning coastal views before your day begins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dietary Needs & Special Meal Requests

We understand that dietary needs vary, and we want every guest to feel comfortable and well cared for at mealtimes. Whether you have a food allergy, follow a specific diet, or simply prefer certain ingredients, our team is happy to discuss options and do our best to accommodate your requests.

<Card
  title="Contact Us About Dining"
  icon="envelope"
  href="/contact"
>
  Reach out to our team before your stay to discuss dietary restrictions, food allergies, or any special meal requests. We're here to make sure your dining experience is as enjoyable as everything else about your visit.
</Card>

<Warning>
  If you have a severe food allergy or a medically required dietary restriction, please contact us directly before your arrival so we can take the appropriate steps to prepare. Do not rely solely on the Special Requests booking field for critical allergy information.
</Warning>
