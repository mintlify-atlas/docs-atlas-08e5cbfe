Travlr Getaways is a beach resort website built to make coastal trip planning straightforward. Whether you're looking for a quiet room near the water, a fresh meal at the resort, or an afternoon on the reef, every section of the site is organized so that the details you want most are easy to reach from a single place. The goal is a relaxed planning experience that matches the kind of stay you're actually looking for.

<Info>
  The homepage puts it plainly: "Explore a relaxed coastal retreat built around comfortable rooms, fresh meals, and days spent on the water. Compare accommodations, preview the dining options, and use the travel guide to plan a stay that fits the way you like to unwind."
</Info>

## What You'll Find Here

The site is organized into six main sections, each focused on a specific part of your stay. Here's a quick overview of what each one covers:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rooms">
    Browse available room categories, compare what each includes, and select the accommodation style that suits your travel plans. Options range from comfortable standard rooms to coastal-view selections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Travel & Destinations">
    Explore reef-inspired destinations and water-focused excursions. This section highlights the coastal activities available during your stay, including guided reef visits and beach outings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meals">
    Preview the resort dining options before you arrive. The meals section lets you review available food choices so there are no surprises when it comes time to eat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Travel Guide">
    Read practical articles on water safety and responsible reef recreation. The guide links to authoritative sources from the National Park Service and NOAA to help you plan with confidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Booking">
    Once you've compared rooms and reviewed the options, the booking page is where your stay comes together. Reserve your room and finalize your visit in one straightforward step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact">
    Have questions about accommodations, meals, accessibility, or activities? The contact page connects you directly with the guest-services team before you commit to a booking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A Coastal Focus

<Tip>
  The resort's beach access and reef excursions are central to the Travlr Getaways experience. Before heading out on the water, check the travel guide for current safety tips and reef-friendly recreation practices. A little preparation makes for a much better day.
</Tip>

Every part of the Travlr Getaways site is designed to support the activities that coastal travel is really about — swimming, exploring reefs, relaxing on the beach, and eating well after a day outdoors. The rooms, meals, and destinations sections are intentionally connected so you can move from browsing to booking without losing the thread of your plans.

<Note>
  If this is your first visit, a good starting point is the **Rooms** overview to get a sense of what's available, followed by the **Travel & Destinations** section to see what you can do during your stay.
</Note>
